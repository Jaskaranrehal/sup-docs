You set these on the **Channels & members** step when you
[create](/docs/follow-ups/create) or [edit](/docs/follow-ups/manage#edit-a-follow-up) a
follow-up.

![The Channels & members step: the #standup channel with threads on, eight members, and the advanced settings open](/images/follow-ups/create-channels.png)

## Channel

Sup! posts each member's response to this channel. A follow-up can also run without a
channel; its responses are then only on the follow-up's page in the web app.

<Tabs items={['Slack', 'Microsoft Teams', 'Webex']}>
<Tab value="Slack">

1. Keep **Add channel** on and type the channel name.
2. Select **Send test notification**. Sup! posts a test message to the channel. The channel
   is only added once this succeeds.

Sup! has to be in the channel. If it isn't, you'll see *"Sup bot is not present in channel…"*;
add `@sup` to the channel and try again. For private channels, follow the steps in **How to add
Sup to a private channel** on the same page.

</Tab>
<Tab value="Microsoft Teams">

Pick the channel under **Add channel**.

</Tab>
<Tab value="Webex">

Pick the channel under **Add channel**.

</Tab>

### Share responses as a thread

Turn this on to collect the day's responses in a thread instead of separate channel messages.
Sup! starts the thread with your [thread message](/docs/follow-ups/messages#thread-message).
It needs a channel, and is always on when
[Send responses at a specific time](#send-responses-at-a-specific-time) is on.

### Sync all members of the channel (Slack)

Makes everyone in the channel a member of the follow-up, and keeps the list in step as people
join or leave the channel. Sup! must be in the channel for this to work.

## Members

Search for people by name and add them one at a time. Members are the people who get the
follow-up and answer it.

On the free plan the number of members is limited, and the page shows the limit. On a trial,
only the first members up to the free plan's limit are kept when the trial ends.

## Advanced settings

### Who can view and who can edit

Controls who can see the follow-up and its responses, and who can change it. See
[Who can create, view and edit](/docs/follow-ups/access#who-can-view-and-edit-a-follow-up).

### Send responses at a specific time

By default, each response is posted to the channel as soon as it's submitted. Turn this on to
hold them and post all of them at one time. Pick it from **Select a time slot**; the options are
half-hourly from the follow-up's time up to 11:30 PM.

This needs a channel, posts the responses in a thread, and isn't available when
[User timezone](/docs/follow-ups/schedule#timezones) is on or when the follow-up's time is
11:30 PM or later.

### Receive daily summary

On by default. Sup! posts a [summary](/docs/follow-ups/stats-and-summaries#daily-summary) to
the channel two hours after the follow-up's time, or along with the responses if you send them
at a specific time. When responses go in a thread, choose **Channel** or **Thread** for where
the summary is posted.
