---
title: Create a follow-up
description: Set up a new follow-up from a template or from scratch.
---

<Callout title="Before you start">
  If your workspace admins have turned on access control, only admins with the **Create
  follow-ups** permission can create one. See [Who can create, view and
  edit](/docs/follow-ups/access). Your plan also limits how many follow-ups can be active at
  once.
</Callout>

<Steps>
<Step>

### Start a new follow-up

In the [Sup! dashboard](https://app.supbot.com/dashboard/followups), select **Follow-ups** in the
sidebar, then **Create follow-up**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Choose a template

Select a template card to start from it, or select **Create your own** to start blank. Each
card lists the template's questions. A template fills in the name, active days, frequency,
questions and mood tracking, and you can change any of them in the next steps.

</Step>
<Step>

### Choose the interaction type (Slack only)

Choose how members answer, then select **Next**:

- **Conversational** (the default): members answer by sending Sup! a message, one question at a
  time.
- **Dialog window**: members fill in a form.

The preview below the options shows what members will see.

![The interaction type step with Conversational selected and a preview of the conversation in Slack](/images/follow-ups/create-type.png)

If the Sup! app needs to be reinstalled before you can use conversational follow-ups, a
**Reinstall** link appears here.

</Step>
<Step>

### Name & schedule

Give the follow-up a **Name**, then set its **Time**, **Frequency**, **Active days** and
**Reminders**. This step also has the **Mood tracking** switch.
[More on scheduling](/docs/follow-ups/schedule).

</Step>
<Step>

### Channels & members

Pick the channel where responses are posted and add the members who answer. Under **Advanced
settings** you can also set who can view and edit the follow-up, post responses at a set time
and turn the daily summary on or off. [More on channels and members](/docs/follow-ups/channel-and-members).

</Step>
<Step>

### Add questions

The follow-up starts with two required text questions. Edit them, add more, change their answer
types or load a template's questions. [More on questions](/docs/follow-ups/questions).

</Step>
<Step>

### Messages & responses

Customize the messages Sup! sends, and choose whether members can say they're not working
today, edit their response or answer for past dates.
[More on messages](/docs/follow-ups/messages).

Select **Create follow-up** to finish.

</Step>
</Steps>

You can move between steps with **Back** and **Next**. Once the follow-up is created, the app
opens its page. The follow-up first runs at its next scheduled time. To send it right away, use
[Trigger now](/docs/follow-ups/manage#trigger-now).

<Callout type="warn">
  On a trial, a banner on this page warns you if the new follow-up will be deactivated when the
  trial ends because the free plan allows fewer follow-ups.
</Callout>
