---
title: Schedule and reminders
description: When a follow-up runs, how often, in which timezone, and how Sup! reminds people.
---

You set these on the **Name & schedule** step when you
[create](/docs/follow-ups/create) or [edit](/docs/follow-ups/manage#edit-a-follow-up) a
follow-up.

## Time and frequency

- **Time**: when members get the follow-up, in 15-minute steps.
- **Active days**: the weekdays it runs on.
- **Frequency**:
  - **Weekly** (the default): every selected day, every week.
  - **Bi-weekly**: every other week.
  - **First selected day of the month** or **Last selected day of the month**: once a month.

Bi-weekly and monthly frequencies are paid features and run on a single weekday. Choosing one
of them keeps only one of your active days.

## Reminders

**Reminders** sends members who haven't answered yet a nudge: **No reminder** (the default),
**Once**, **Twice** or **Thrice**. Once you turn reminders on, choose an **Interval** of
**15 mins**, **30 mins** or **60 mins**. A note under the field shows exactly when the reminders
go out.

The reminder says *"Your response for (follow-up name) is pending."* Members who started a
conversational follow-up but didn't finish are told their response is incomplete.

To remind people yourself, see [Send reminders](/docs/follow-ups/responses#send-reminders).

## Timezones

A follow-up runs in your workspace timezone unless you change it:

- **Custom timezone**, under **Advanced settings**: run in a different timezone. This is a paid
  feature.
- **User timezone** (Slack only): each member gets the follow-up at the chosen time in their own
  timezone. This is a paid feature. When it's on, **Custom timezone** and
  [Send responses at a specific time](/docs/follow-ups/channel-and-members#send-responses-at-a-specific-time)
  are turned off.

## Mood tracking

Turn on **Mood tracking** to ask members how they feel at the end of each response. Moods are
anonymous. See [Mood tracking](/docs/follow-ups/stats-and-summaries#mood-tracking).

## Leave and public holidays

If your workspace uses Sup! leave tracking, follow-ups take it into account:

- Members on approved full-day leave or a public holiday aren't asked that day. Their response is
  recorded as on leave.
- If it's a public holiday for the follow-up, that day's run is skipped.
- Half-day leaves are listed in the daily summary.

There's no setting for this; it's always on.
