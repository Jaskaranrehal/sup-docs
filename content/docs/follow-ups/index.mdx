---
title: Overview
description: Scheduled check-ins that collect updates from your team in Slack, Microsoft Teams or Webex.
---

A follow-up is a set of questions Sup! asks your team on a schedule: a daily standup, a weekly
check-in, an end-of-day update. When it runs, each member gets a message from Sup! in chat and
answers there. Sup! can post the answers to a channel, and keeps all of them in the web app.

## How a follow-up runs

<Steps>
<Step>

### Sup! messages each member

At the scheduled time, every member gets your intro message with three buttons: **Yes**,
**Use previous response** and **Not working today**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Members answer

They answer in a form, or on Slack, one question at a time in the conversation. If you set up
reminders, Sup! nudges anyone who hasn't answered yet.

</Step>
<Step>

### Answers are shared

Each response is posted to the follow-up's channel. You can choose to post each one on its own,
in a thread, or all of them together at a set time.

</Step>
<Step>

### You get a summary

Two hours after the start time, Sup! posts a daily summary showing who answered, who hasn't, who
is off and the team's average mood.

</Step>
</Steps>

The follow-up's page in the [web app](https://app.supbot.com/dashboard/followups) has
everything too: responses by day, stats and downloadable reports.

## Find your follow-ups

Select **Follow-ups** in the left sidebar of the web app. The list has these columns:

- **Name**, with a **Mood** or **Summary** chip if those are turned on
- **Schedule**, the time and frequency, with the active days lit
- **Channel**, where responses are posted
- **Members** and **Manager**
- **Active**, a switch to pause or resume the follow-up

Select a row to open the follow-up. Use the **⋯** menu on a row to **Delete** or **Duplicate**
it. Paused follow-ups are hidden until you select **Show inactive**.

You only see the follow-ups you're allowed to view. See
[Who can create, view and edit](/docs/follow-ups/access).

## Dialog window or conversational

On Slack you choose how members answer when you create the follow-up:

| | Dialog window | Conversational |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How members answer | Fill in a form that pops up | Sup! asks one question at a time; they reply by typing |
| Platforms | Slack, Microsoft Teams, Webex | Slack only |
| **User** question type | Yes (Slack and Teams) | No |

Microsoft Teams and Webex always use a form, so they skip this choice.
