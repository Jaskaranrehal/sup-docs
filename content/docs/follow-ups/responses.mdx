---
title: Read responses and send reminders
description: See who answered, read responses by day or by person, and remind people who haven't answered.
---

Open a follow-up from the **Follow-ups** list. Its page has two tabs, **Responses** and
[**Stats**](/docs/follow-ups/stats-and-summaries#stats), with a **Details** panel on the side.
On narrow screens, **Details** is a third tab.

![The Engineering standup page on Friday, October 23: mood 4.2/5, 5 of 8 responded, two blockers reported, and the Details panel](/images/follow-ups/detail.png)

## The Details panel

The panel lists the follow-up's **Channel**, **Members**, **Schedule** (with the next run date),
**Mood tracking**, **Summary** and **Questions**. Select any of them to
[edit that part](/docs/follow-ups/manage#edit-a-follow-up).

## Responses

The **Responses** tab shows one day at a time. Pick the day with the date picker.

- **List view** groups answers by question. The blocker question shows how many blockers were
  reported that day.
- **Card view** shows one card per member.
- **Filter members** narrows it to specific people.

Above the responses:

- **Average team mood**, if mood tracking is on. Moods submitted in the last hour aren't
  counted yet.
- **Responded**: how many members have answered.
- **Haven't responded yet**: who is still pending. Select it to see the list and send
  reminders.
- **Not working today**: who said they're off, if that option is on.

To see everything one person has answered, select them in the members list. Their page shows
their responses month by month.

![Liam Chen's responses to Engineering standup for October 2026, one card per day](/images/follow-ups/member.png)

## Send reminders

<Steps>
<Step>
On the **Responses** tab, select **Haven't responded yet**.
</Step>
<Step>
Select **Remind** next to a person, or **Remind all** at the bottom.
</Step>
</Steps>

![The Members that haven't responded yet dialog with Remind buttons for two people and Remind all](/images/follow-ups/pending.png)

The person gets a message in chat saying who is reminding them, with a link to answer. People
on leave show **On leave** instead of **Remind**.

Sending reminders is a paid feature. It needs edit access to the follow-up, and the follow-up
must be active.

## Add your own response

If you're a member, **Add response** on the **Responses** tab opens the form for the selected
date. See [Answer a follow-up](/docs/follow-ups/answering#answer-in-the-web-app).
