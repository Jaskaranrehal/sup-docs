---
title: Generate a report
description: Download a follow-up's responses as an Excel file.
---

<Steps>
<Step>
Open the follow-up and select **Generate report** on the **Responses** tab.
</Step>
<Step>
Under **Members**, pick people, or leave it as **All members**.
</Step>
<Step>
Under **Response date**, set **From date** and **To date**. To date starts as today.
</Step>
<Step>
Choose a **Report layout**:

- **Separate tabs for each member** (the default): one sheet per person.
- **Separate tabs for each week**: one sheet per week.
- **One tab for all members**: everything on one sheet.
</Step>
<Step>
Select **Generate report**. The file downloads as `<follow-up name>.xlsx`.
</Step>
</Steps>

Each row is one response, with its date and submission time, an answer for each question
(optional questions are marked **(optional)**), and whether the person was on holiday.

<Callout>On the free plan, reports only cover the last 30 days.</Callout>

## Google Sheets

On a paid plan you can also send responses to Google Sheets. Under **Integrations** in the web
app, select the follow-ups to sync. Sup! creates a new sheet at the end of every month with all
of that month's submissions.
