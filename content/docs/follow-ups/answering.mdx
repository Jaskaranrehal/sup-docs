---
title: Answer a follow-up
description: For members, how to answer a follow-up right in Slack or Microsoft Teams, and how to edit a response.
---

You answer a follow-up where you already work. At the follow-up's time, Sup! messages you in
Slack or Microsoft Teams, and you reply right there. You don't need to open the Sup! dashboard.

## Answer in Slack or Microsoft Teams

The message from Sup! has three buttons:

![A Sup! message in Slack asking for updates, with Yes, Use previous response and Not working today buttons](/images/follow-ups/chat-message.png)

- **Yes**: answer today's questions.
- **Use previous response**: open the form already filled in with your last answers, then
  change what you need and submit.
- **Not working today**: tell Sup! you're off. Sup! replies *"I have marked you as not working
  today."* and lets the channel know. This button only appears if the follow-up allows it.

What happens after **Yes** depends on where you are:

<Tabs items={['Slack', 'Microsoft Teams']}>
<Tab value="Slack">

The follow-up's creator picks one of two ways to answer.

**Dialog window**: **Yes** opens a form with all the questions.

1. Answer the questions. If you have a blocker, tick **Do you have any blockers?** and describe it.
2. If the follow-up tracks mood, pick an answer for **How are you feeling today?**
3. Select **Submit**.

**Conversational**: after **Yes**, Sup! asks the questions one at a time in your direct
messages, and you answer by typing your reply.

- Optional questions have a **Skip** button.
- **View previous answers** shows what you said last time.
- For the blocker question, reply "no" or "none" if you have nothing blocking you.

To answer for a particular day, send Sup! a direct message:
`response <follow-up-name> <YYYY-MM-DD>`. Type `help` to see how.

</Tab>
<Tab value="Microsoft Teams">

**Yes** opens a pop-up form with all the questions.

1. Answer the questions. Leave the blocker question blank if nothing is blocking you.
2. If the follow-up tracks mood, pick an answer for your mood.
3. Select **Submit**.

</Tab>
</Tabs>

After you submit, Sup! sends the follow-up's thank-you message and posts your answers to the
channel, if the follow-up has one.

<Callout>
  If you start a conversational follow-up and don't finish, Sup! posts what you answered to the
  channel about two hours later, marked as an incomplete response.
</Callout>

The first time you're added to a follow-up, Sup! also introduces itself and tells you when the
follow-up runs and where your answers will be posted.

## Edit a response

If the follow-up allows editing, the thank-you message has an **Edit response** button:

- **Slack, dialog window**: opens the form again, with an **On leave?** checkbox. Select
  **Update**.
- **Slack conversational and Microsoft Teams**: opens your response in the Sup! dashboard.

In a Slack conversational follow-up you can also edit or delete your own reply message.

## Reminders

If you haven't answered, Sup! may message you *"Your response for (follow-up name) is
pending."* These are the follow-up's automatic reminders. A manager can also remind you from
the dashboard; that message says who is reminding you and links straight to the response form.

## Alternatively, submit from the dashboard

You can also answer from the Sup! dashboard, for example to catch up on a day you missed.

<Steps>
<Step>
Open the follow-up from **Follow-ups** in the dashboard and pick the date.
</Step>
<Step>
Select **Add response**. You see the same three buttons as the chat message.
</Step>
<Step>
Select **Yes**, answer the questions, then select **Submit response**. Turn on **Do you have
any blockers?** if something is blocking you.
</Step>
</Steps>

![The Submit response page for Engineering standup on Oct 23, 2026, with two text questions, the blockers switch, an Options question and the mood question](/images/follow-ups/respond.png)

Earlier dates are only available if the follow-up lets members answer for past dates. Your own
answers on the follow-up's page have an **Edit** link.

On Slack, the Sup! app's **Home** tab also lists **Your follow-ups** with the same buttons.
