## Stats

Open the follow-up and select the **Stats** tab. Choose a period: **This week**, **Past week**,
**This month**, **Past month**, **This year** or **Past year**. The year options need a paid
plan. Periods from before the follow-up started show **Not enough data**.

Need a stat that isn't there? Select **Request a custom stat**.

## Daily summary

If **Receive daily summary** is on (it is by default), Sup! posts a summary to the follow-up's
channel two hours after the follow-up's time. If responses are
[sent at a specific time](/docs/follow-ups/channel-and-members#send-responses-at-a-specific-time),
the summary goes out with them. The summary is updated when late responses come in.

The summary includes:

- **Members on leave**
- **Pending members**, with a **View in dashboard** link that opens the list of people who
  haven't answered
- **Response submitted**
- **Average mood**, if mood tracking is on
- **Weekly submission rate**, on the last active day of the week

Buttons at the bottom open the follow-up and its insights in the dashboard.

Turn the summary on or off under **Advanced settings** on the **Channels & members** step.

For the team's mood and the weekly mood chart, see [Mood tracking](/docs/follow-ups/mood-tracking).
