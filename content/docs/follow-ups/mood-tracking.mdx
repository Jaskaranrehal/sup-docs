---
title: Mood tracking
description: Ask members how they feel with each response, anonymously, and follow the team's mood.
---

With mood tracking on, every response to a follow-up ends with one more question: **How are you
feeling today?** Members pick one of five moods. Nobody sees an individual's mood, managers
included. You only ever see the team's average.

## Turn it on for a follow-up

On the **Name & schedule** step of a follow-up, turn on **Mood tracking**. It's available on
every plan, and you can turn it on or off at any time by
[editing the follow-up](/docs/follow-ups/manage#edit-a-follow-up).

Follow-ups with mood tracking on show a **Mood** chip in the **Follow-ups** list.

## What members see

After the last question, members are asked **How are you feeling today?** and pick a mood from
**Select your mood**:

- In a **Slack dialog window** or a **Microsoft Teams** form, it's the last field before
  **Submit**.
- In a **Slack conversational** follow-up, Sup! asks it after the last question.
- On the dashboard's **Submit response** page, it's the last question, with a reminder that
  mood tracking data is anonymous.

![The mood question at the end of the Submit response page, with the note that mood tracking data is anonymous](/images/follow-ups/mood-question.png)

## See the team's mood

- **Responses tab**: open the follow-up and pick a day. **Average team mood** shows the
  average out of 5 with a matching face, or your own mood name if you've renamed them.
- **Daily summary**: the summary Sup! posts to the channel includes **Average mood**.

![The stats strip on a follow-up's Responses tab: average team mood 4.2/5, 5 of 8 responded, 2 haven't responded, 1 not working today](/images/follow-ups/mood-average.png)

<Callout title="Kept anonymous">
  Moods aren't shown per person anywhere: not on the responses page, in the channel, or in
  [reports](/docs/follow-ups/reports). The average also leaves out moods submitted in the last
  hour, so a single new mood can't be singled out.
</Callout>

## Weekly mood summary

On a paid plan, Sup! can post a chart of the week's mood to the follow-up's channel. It goes
out on the follow-up's last active day of the week, two hours after its scheduled time, when:

- the follow-up is **Weekly** and has mood tracking on,
- it doesn't [send responses at a specific time](/docs/follow-ups/channel-and-members#send-responses-at-a-specific-time), and
- **Receive weekly mood summary** is on in the workspace's mood settings (it is by default).

If the follow-up's summary goes in a thread, the mood summary does too. The message has a
link to stop receiving it.

## Mood settings

Mood settings apply to every follow-up in the workspace. In the dashboard, go to **Settings**
and open the **Mood** tab.

![The Mood settings tab: Receive weekly mood summary and Customize mood names switches, and the five mood names](/images/follow-ups/mood-settings.png)

- **Receive weekly mood summary**: turn the [weekly chart](#weekly-mood-summary) on or off.
- **Customize mood names**: rename the five moods to match how your team talks. The list starts
  with the best mood. The defaults, from best to worst, are Excited, Happy, Neutral, Sad and
  Disappointed.

Select **Save settings** when you're done. Both settings are paid features; on the free plan
the tab shows **This is a paid feature** with an **Upgrade** link.

If your workspace uses access control, only workspace admins can open the **Mood** tab. See
[Who can create, view and edit](/docs/follow-ups/access).
