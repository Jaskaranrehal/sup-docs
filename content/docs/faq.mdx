## Getting started

<Accordions type="multiple">
<Accordion title="Which chat apps does Sup! work with?" id="chat-apps">
Slack and Microsoft Teams. A few features are Slack only, such as conversational follow-ups and
setting a Slack status while someone is on leave. Each page says so where it applies.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="How do I log in to the dashboard?" id="log-in">
Go to [app.supbot.com](https://app.supbot.com) and select **Login with Slack** or
**Login with Teams**, whichever your team uses. See [Get started](/docs#get-started).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="I'm in more than one workspace. How do I switch?" id="switch-workspace">
Open **Settings** and select the workspace card at the bottom of the tab list. Pick another
workspace you've signed in to, or add one. See [Settings](/docs/settings#switch-workspace-or-log-out).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Do my teammates need to use the dashboard?" id="dashboard-needed">
No. Members answer follow-ups, fill in forms and get leave updates in Slack or Microsoft Teams.
The dashboard is where you set things up, and where anyone can answer or request leave if they
prefer.
</Accordion>
</Accordions>

## Admins and access

<Accordions type="multiple">
<Accordion title="Who can change Sup!'s settings?" id="who-can-change-settings">
By default, everyone in the workspace. To limit this to a few people, turn on **Access control**
in **Settings > Admin** and choose what each admin can do. See
[Admins and access control](/docs/settings/admins).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="It says I don't have permission. What do I do?" id="no-permission">
Your workspace uses access control and you aren't an admin for that part of Sup!. Select
**Request admin access** to ask your admins. See
[People without access](/docs/settings/admins#people-without-access).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="How do I make someone else an admin?" id="add-admin">
In **Settings > Admin**, select **Add user**, pick them, untick what they shouldn't do, and select
**Save settings**. You need the **Manage admins** permission. See
[Turn on access control](/docs/settings/admins#turn-on-access-control).
</Accordion>
</Accordions>

## Members and syncing

<Accordions type="multiple">
<Accordion title="Someone who just joined isn't showing up in Sup!" id="missing-member">
Go to **Settings > Workspace** and select **Sync workspace data**. Sup! updates the workspace's
details and members. You can sync once every six hours. See
[Sync workspace data](/docs/settings/workspace#sync-workspace-data).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="My name or profile picture in Sup! is out of date" id="profile-out-of-date">
On Slack, Sup! syncs names and profile images from Slack every weekend, so change them in Slack.
To update them sooner, an admin can select **Sync workspace data** in **Settings > Workspace**.
</Accordion>
</Accordions>

## Follow-ups

<Accordions type="multiple">
<Accordion title="Why did a follow-up arrive at the wrong time?" id="wrong-time">
Follow-ups run in the workspace timezone unless the follow-up sets its own. Check
**Workspace timezone** in **Settings > Workspace**, and the follow-up's
[timezone settings](/docs/follow-ups/schedule#timezones). On Slack, a follow-up can also run in
each member's own timezone, which they set in **Settings > Profile**.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Sup! can't post in my channel" id="cant-post">
Sup! has to be in the channel. On Slack, add `@sup` to the channel and select **Send test
notification** again. Private channels need a few extra steps, shown in the dashboard. See
[Channel](/docs/follow-ups/channel-and-members#channel).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="A follow-up stopped running" id="stopped-running">
Someone may have paused it, or Sup! paused it automatically because nobody was using it: it's
more than 15 days old, hasn't been edited in 14 days, and has had no responses in 45 days. Turn
it back on to use it again. See [Pause or resume](/docs/follow-ups/manage#pause-or-resume).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Why wasn't someone asked today?" id="not-asked">
If your workspace uses the Leave Tracker, people on approved full-day leave or a public holiday
aren't asked, and their response is recorded as on leave. See
[Leave and public holidays](/docs/follow-ups/schedule#leave-and-public-holidays).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Can members answer without Slack or Microsoft Teams?" id="answer-from-dashboard">
Yes. They can also submit their response from the dashboard. See
[Alternatively, submit from the dashboard](/docs/follow-ups/answering#alternatively-submit-from-the-dashboard).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Who can see the responses?" id="who-sees-responses">
Each follow-up has its own **Who can view?** setting. It's **Everyone** by default; on a paid
plan you can limit it to the follow-up's members, chosen managers or workspace admins. See
[Who can create, view and edit](/docs/follow-ups/access).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Is mood tracking anonymous?" id="mood-anonymous">
Yes. Moods aren't shown per person anywhere, only as a team average. See
[Mood tracking](/docs/follow-ups/mood-tracking).
</Accordion>
</Accordions>

## Leave Tracker

<Accordions type="multiple">
<Accordion title="How do people request leave?" id="request-leave">
On Slack, from the Sup! app's **Home** tab with **Create holiday / leave**. On Microsoft Teams,
from the dashboard under **Holidays / Leaves**. See [Request leave](/docs/leave-tracker/request-leave).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Who approves leave?" id="who-approves">
The managers of the person's team. They get a direct message and can approve or reject it right
there. See [Approvals](/docs/leave-tracker/approvals) and
[Members and managers](/docs/leave-tracker/set-up-a-team#members-and-managers).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Why does a leave count fewer days than I expected?" id="days-counted">
Leave only counts the workspace's working days. Team and public holidays inside it aren't
charged, and half days count as half. See
[How days are counted](/docs/leave-tracker/leave-types-and-policy#how-days-are-counted).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="How do I make sure people get the right public holidays?" id="public-holidays">
Public holidays come from each person's location. Add your locations and place people in them, or
let people pick their own in **Settings > Profile**. See [Locations](/docs/leave-tracker/locations).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Can I see leave in my own calendar?" id="calendar">
Yes. Select **Subscribe** on the **Calendar** tab to get a link for Google Calendar, Apple Calendar
or Outlook. See [Subscribe in your calendar app](/docs/leave-tracker/leaves-and-calendar#subscribe-in-your-calendar-app).
</Accordion>
</Accordions>

## Forms

<Accordions type="multiple">
<Accordion title="Can a form be anonymous?" id="anonymous-form">
Yes. Turn on **Anonymous responses** when you create the form. It hides who answered everywhere,
and can't be changed later. See [Create a form](/docs/forms/create).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="What's the difference between a form and a follow-up?" id="form-vs-follow-up">
A follow-up runs on a schedule and asks its members every time. A form has no schedule: people
fill it in whenever they need to, such as a request or a one-time survey. See [Forms](/docs/forms).
</Accordion>
</Accordions>

## Plans and billing

<Accordions type="multiple">
<Accordion title="Which features are paid?" id="paid-features">
Each page says when a feature needs a paid plan. To see or change your plans, open **Billing** in
the dashboard's left sidebar. With access control on, only admins with **Manage billing** can
change them.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Can I get a discount by referring another team?" id="referral">
On Slack, yes. Share your link from **Settings > Referral Program**: you get 2 months off when
they subscribe, and they get 30% off for 3 months. See [Referral Program](/docs/settings/referral-program).
</Accordion>
</Accordions>
