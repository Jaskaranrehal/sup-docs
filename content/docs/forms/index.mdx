title: Overview

---

A form is a set of questions your team answers on demand: a lunch poll, an IT equipment
request, an anonymous survey. Unlike a [follow-up](/docs/follow-ups), a form has no schedule.
You share it once, or pin it somewhere people can find it, and people fill it in from Slack or
Microsoft Teams when they need to.

Responses can be posted to a channel, sent to the people who need to know, and marked as done
with an [approval flow](/docs/forms/responses#approvals).

<Callout>Forms are a paid feature. On plans without them, the Forms page explains what they do and offers an **Upgrade** button.</Callout>

## Find your forms

Select **Forms** in the dashboard's left sidebar.

![The Forms list with three active forms, their descriptions, response counts and Active switches](/images/forms/list.png)

The list shows each form's **Name**, **Description**, number of **Responses** and an **Active**
switch. Select a row to open the form. Use the **⋯** menu to **Delete** or **Duplicate** it.
Inactive forms are hidden until you select **Show inactive**.

You see the forms whose responses everyone can view, and the forms you created yourself. See
[Who can see a form](/docs/forms/manage#who-can-see-and-manage-a-form).
