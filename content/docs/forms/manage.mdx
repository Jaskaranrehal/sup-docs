---
title: Edit, deactivate, duplicate or delete
description: Change a form, stop it taking responses, copy it, or remove it, and who can do each.
---

## Edit a form

Open the form and select **Edit** to go through its steps again, then **Update form**. To
change one part, select it in the **Details** panel instead.

- **Anonymous responses** can't be changed after the form is created.
- Only the form's creator can change **Who can view the responses?**
- Removing a question keeps its past answers in responses and reports.

## Deactivate or activate

Use the **Active** switch in the **Forms** list, or the **Active** label on the form's page.
When you deactivate, confirm with **Deactivate**: people can no longer submit responses, and
the form can't be shared and leaves the pinned lists. Turn it back on at any time.

## Duplicate

From the **⋯** menu, select **Duplicate** and confirm. The copy is named *"(name) copy"*, has the
same settings and questions but no responses, and opens in the editor so you can review it
before sharing.

## Delete

From the **⋯** menu, select **Delete**, then **Delete permanently**.

<Callout type="error">
  Deleting removes the form and every response to it. This can't be undone. If you might need
  the responses, [generate a report](/docs/forms/responses#generate-a-report) first, or
  deactivate the form instead.
</Callout>

## Who can see and manage a form

**Who can view the responses?** decides who can open the form in the dashboard:

| Setting | Who can view, edit, share and approve |
| --- | --- |
| **Everyone** | Everyone in the workspace |
| **Only me** | Only the form's creator |

Either way, anyone in the workspace can fill out the form, and only the person who submitted a
response can edit it.
