title: Share and pin a form
description: Send a form to a channel or to people, copy its link, or pin it where people can find it.
---

## Share a form

Open the form and select **Share** (it's also the last step of creating a form).

![The Share form step: Copy link, and Share form using Slack with Channel and Members options](/images/forms/share.png)

- **Share form using link**: select **Copy link** and paste it anywhere. The link opens the
  form in the dashboard.
- **Share form using Slack** (or **Teams**): choose **Channel** or **Members**, pick where it
  goes, and select **Continue**.

Sup! posts a message saying who created the form and asking people to fill it in, with a
**Fill out form** button. For anonymous forms, the message says responses are anonymous.

Only active forms can be shared.

## Pin a form

Pinning keeps a form one click away, without sending a message. Open the form and select the
pin icon (**Pin this form to Slack home & Dashboard**). A pinned form appears:

- in the **Pinned forms** section of the Sup! app's **Home** tab in Slack or Microsoft Teams,
- in **Pinned forms** on the dashboard's home page.

Select the pin icon again to unpin it. Deactivated forms drop out of the pinned lists.
