---
title: Fill out a form
description: For members, how to fill out a form right in Slack or Microsoft Teams, and how to edit your response.
---

You fill out a form where you already work: select **Fill out form** on the message Sup!
sends to your channel or your direct messages.

## Fill it out in Slack or Microsoft Teams

<Tabs items={['Slack', 'Microsoft Teams']}>
<Tab value="Slack">

1. Select **Fill out form**. A dialog opens with the form's questions.
2. Answer the questions. Optional questions say so.
3. Select **Submit**.

</Tab>
<Tab value="Microsoft Teams">

1. Select **Fill out form**. A pop-up opens with the form's questions.
2. Answer the questions.
3. Select **Submit**. The pop-up confirms your response was submitted.

</Tab>
</Tabs>

Afterwards, Sup! sends you *"Thank you for submitting your response for (form name)."* with an
**Edit response** button. If the form posts to a channel, your response appears there too,
under your name, or as anonymous if the form is anonymous.

If the form is no longer active, or you've already answered a form that takes one response per
person, Sup! tells you instead of opening the form.

## Pinned forms

Forms your team uses often may be pinned. Open the Sup! app's **Home** tab in Slack or
Microsoft Teams and look under **Pinned forms**. Each pinned form opens in the dashboard.

## Edit your response

Select **Edit response** on Sup!'s thank-you message, or **Edit** on your response in the
dashboard. Change your answers and select **Update response**. If the form posts to a channel,
the post is updated.

You can't edit a response after it has been approved.

## Alternatively, fill it out from the dashboard

You can also answer from the Sup! dashboard:

- On the dashboard's home page, select **Respond** next to a form under **Pinned forms**.
- Open a form's shared link.
- Open the form and select **Add response**.

![The Pinned forms list on the dashboard home page, with Respond buttons](/images/forms/pinned.png)

Answer the questions, then select **Submit response**.
