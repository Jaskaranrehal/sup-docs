---
title: Responses, approvals and reports
description: Read a form's responses, mark them done with the approval flow, and download them.
---

Open the form from **Forms**. The **Responses** tab lists every response as a card, newest
first, with a **Details** panel beside it.

![The IT equipment request form: four response cards, three with a Mark as ordered button and one Completed, and the Details panel](/images/forms/detail.png)

- **Filter members** shows responses from particular people. Anonymous forms don't have it.
- With an approval flow, filter by **All responses**, **Approved responses** or
  **Non-approved responses**.
- Select a card to open the response on its own page, with who submitted it and when.

The **Details** panel shows the channel responses go to, whether the form is anonymous,
whether people can respond more than once, the approval flow, and the questions. Select any of
them to edit it.

## Approvals

If the form uses an approval flow, each response has a button with your approval text, for
example **Mark as ordered**. Select it and confirm.

![The Approve this response? dialog with Cancel and Mark as ordered](/images/forms/approve.png)

The response then shows **Completed**, and the person who submitted it gets a direct message
saying it was approved, with a **View submission** button. Approved responses can't be edited.

Approving is one way: there's no reject or undo.

## Notifications

Depending on the form's [notification settings](/docs/forms/create#notifications):

- each response is posted to the form's channel, in a thread if threads are on, and updated
  when it's edited;
- the people you chose get a direct message, *"New submission on the (form) form."* or
  *"Submission updated on the (form) form."*, with a **View response** button.

## Stats

The **Stats** tab collects the answers question by question: a list of answers for text,
number and date questions (with a **Total** when all answers are numbers), the people picked for
user questions, and a chart for options questions.

## Generate a report

On the **Responses** tab, select **Generate report**. The form downloads as an Excel file,
`<form name>.xlsx`, with one row per response: who submitted it (or *User* on anonymous forms),
the date, and a column for each question. Optional questions are marked **(optional)**.
Questions you've since removed are included, so no answers are lost.
