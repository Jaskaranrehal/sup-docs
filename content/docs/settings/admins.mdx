---
title: Admins and access control
description: Choose who can manage admins, follow-ups, holidays and leave, and billing.
---

By default, everyone in the workspace can manage all of Sup!'s settings. Turn on access control
to give that power only to the people you pick, and decide what each of them can do.

Go to **Settings** and open the **Admin** tab.

![The Admin tab with access control on: a table of admins with Manage admins, Create follow-ups, Manage holidays / leaves and Manage billing ticked](/images/settings/admins.png)

## Turn on access control

<Steps>
<Step>
Turn on **Access control**. The admins table appears.
</Step>
<Step>
Select **Add user** and pick someone under **Select member**. A new admin starts with every
permission ticked.
</Step>
<Step>
Untick the permissions they shouldn't have.
</Step>
<Step>
Select **Save settings**.
</Step>
</Steps>

Keep yourself in the list with **Manage admins** ticked. If you don't, Sup! warns you with
**You are not an admin**: once access control is on, you'd lose the option to manage settings.
Select **Enable access control** only if that's what you want.

## Permissions

| Permission | What it allows |
| --- | --- |
| **Manage admins** | Open the Admin tab, add and remove admins, and change their permissions |
| **Create follow-ups** | Create, duplicate and delete follow-ups. See [Who can create, view and edit](/docs/follow-ups/access) |
| **Manage holidays / leaves** | Act as a holiday admin in the Leave Tracker. Only on plans with leave tracking. See [Who can do what](/docs/leave-tracker/access) |
| **Manage billing** | Manage the workspace's plan under **Billing** |

Everyone in the admins list can also open the **Workspace** and **Mood** tabs.

Some ticks can't be changed:

- You can't change your own **Manage admins** permission.
- At least one person has to keep each permission, so the last tick in a column is locked.

## Remove an admin

Select the bin at the end of their row, then **Remove**. The change is saved straight away.

You can't remove yourself, or the last person who holds a permission.

## Turn off access control

Turn off **Access control** and select **Save settings**. Everyone in the workspace can manage
all of Sup!'s settings again.

<Callout>On Slack, Sup! syncs workspace admins from your Slack workspace every weekend.</Callout>

## People without access

People who aren't admins see **You don't have permission to access this page.** on the admin-only
tabs, and a **Permission needed** dialog when they try something only admins can do. Both offer
**Request admin access**, which asks your admins to give them access.
