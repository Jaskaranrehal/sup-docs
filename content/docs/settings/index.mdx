Open **Settings** at the bottom of the dashboard's left sidebar. The settings are split into
tabs, listed down the left of the page.

![The Settings page on the Profile tab, with the Profile, Workspace, Admin, Mood and Referral Program tabs on the left](/images/settings/overview.png)

## The tabs

| Tab | What's in it | Who can open it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Profile](/docs/settings/profile) | Your timezone and your location | Everyone |
| [Workspace](/docs/settings/workspace) | The workspace's timezone and working days, product emails, and syncing members | Everyone, or only admins when access control is on |
| [Admin](/docs/settings/admins) | Access control, and which admins can do what | Everyone, or only admins with **Manage admins** when access control is on |
| [Mood](/docs/follow-ups/mood-tracking#mood-settings) | The weekly mood summary and the mood names | Everyone, or only admins when access control is on |
| [Referral Program](/docs/settings/referral-program) | Your referral link and rewards. Slack only | Everyone |

On a phone, the tabs move into a picker at the top of the page.

## When you can't open a tab

When [access control](/docs/settings/admins) is on and a tab is only for admins, it shows
**You don't have permission to access this page.** Select **Request admin access** to ask your
Sup! admins for access. The button changes to **Request sent**.

## Switch workspace or log out

The card at the bottom of the tab list shows the workspace you're in. Select it to switch to
another workspace you've signed in to, or to add one.

**Logout** signs you out of this workspace.
