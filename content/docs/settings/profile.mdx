Go to **Settings** and open the **Profile** tab. These settings are yours alone: they don't
change anything for anyone else.

![The Profile tab: your name and email, Your timezone, Your location, and Save settings](/images/settings/profile.png)

The card at the top shows your name and email as your workspace has them. On Slack, Sup! syncs
your name and profile image from Slack every weekend, so change them in Slack.

## Your timezone

Reminders and follow-up schedules use this timezone. Pick the timezone you work in.

Follow-ups run in the workspace's timezone by default. See
[Timezones](/docs/follow-ups/schedule#timezones) for when your own timezone is used instead.

## Your location

Your location decides which public holidays you get, and is used for location-wide holidays
and public holiday import. It only shows on plans with leave tracking.

If no locations exist yet, the field says **No locations available to select.** Holiday admins
get an **Add location** link; everyone else is asked to have their workspace managers create one
first. See [Locations](/docs/leave-tracker/locations).

## Save

Select **Save settings**. If you leave the page with unsaved changes, Sup! asks whether you
really want to leave.
