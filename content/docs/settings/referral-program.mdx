---
title: Referral Program
description: Share your referral link and earn free months of Sup! when another team subscribes.
---

The referral program is for Slack workspaces. Go to **Settings** and open the
**Referral Program** tab.

![The Referral Program tab: what you and your referral earn, your referral link with Copy link, and how it works](/images/settings/referral-program.png)

## How it works

<Steps>
<Step>
**Share your link.** Under **Your referral link**, select **Copy link** and send it to teams
who'd like async check-ins.
</Step>
<Step>
**They subscribe.** Your referral signs up with your link and picks any paid plan.
</Step>
<Step>
**You both get rewarded.**
</Step>
</Steps>

| Who | Reward |
| --- | --- |
| You | 2 months of Sup! off your subscription when your referral subscribes to a paid plan |
| Your referral | 30% off for 3 months on any purchase they make |
