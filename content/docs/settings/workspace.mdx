Go to **Settings** and open the **Workspace** tab. These settings apply to everyone in the
workspace. When [access control](/docs/settings/admins) is on, only admins can open this tab.

![The Workspace tab: Workspace timezone, Working days, Product update emails, Save settings and Sync workspace data](/images/settings/workspace.png)

## Workspace timezone

The timezone follow-ups run in, unless a follow-up sets its own. See
[Timezones](/docs/follow-ups/schedule#timezones).

<Callout type="warn">Changing it may affect all existing follow-ups.</Callout>

## Working days

Pick the days your workspace works. The count next to the label shows how many are selected.

The Leave Tracker uses these days: leave only counts working days, and its daily digest goes out
on them. See [How days are counted](/docs/leave-tracker/leave-types-and-policy#how-days-are-counted).

## Product update emails

Turn this on to get occasional news about new Sup! features and improvements.

Select **Save settings** when you're done with any of the above.

## Sync workspace data

Sup! keeps a copy of your workspace's details and members. If someone who just joined Slack or
Microsoft Teams is missing from Sup!, or a name looks out of date, select **Sync workspace data**,
then **Start sync**. This updates the workspace's details and its members.

You can sync once every six hours. Until then the button is greyed out; hover over it to see
when you can sync again.
