## Your balance

On **Overview**, **Your balance** shows how many days you have left this year, and how that adds
up: **Annual allowance**, **Carried over**, **Adjusted**, **Taken**, **Booked** (approved but not
taken yet) and **Pending**. Leave types with their own balance are listed below it. Use the
arrows to look at earlier years.

## Team balances

Approvers see everyone's balance in **Reports > Balances**.

![Reports > Balances: Engineering's balances, with allowance, carried, adjusted, taken, booked, pending and days left for each person](/images/leave-tracker/reports.png)

Days left = allowance + carried over + adjustments − taken − booked. Pending requests are
shown separately.

## Adjust a balance

Select **Adjust** next to a person (in **Reports**, **Settings > Members**, or on their leave's
page).

- **Allowance per year**: give this person a different allowance. Leave it empty to use the
  team's default.
- **Adjustments**: add days (for example, for working a weekend) or take them away with a
  negative number. Add a **Note**; the person can see it.

Select **Save changes**.

## Leave by type

**Reports > By type** shows the days taken or booked in the year, by leave type and month, with
a table of days per member. Pick a month in the chart to narrow the table.

## Export

Approvers can select **Export (year)** and choose **Excel** or **CSV**. The file has one row per
member: allowance, carried over, adjustments, taken, booked, pending, remaining, and days for
each leave type.

**Reports > Analytics** has charts of the team's leave on plans that include holiday stats.
