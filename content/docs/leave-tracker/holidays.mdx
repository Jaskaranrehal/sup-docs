title: Team and public holidays
description: Add the days off a whole team or a whole location gets, and import public holidays.
---

There are two kinds of holiday:

- **Team holidays**: everyone in the team is off, wherever they're based. For example a hack
  day or a team offsite.
- **Public (location) holidays**: everyone based at a [location](/docs/leave-tracker/locations)
  is off, whatever their team.

Each person gets their team's holidays plus their location's.

## The Team & public holidays tab

![The Team & public holidays tab: the Engineering team's holidays, and the New York, London and Bengaluru locations](/images/leave-tracker/holidays.png)

The sentence at the top tells you where your holidays come from and how many days you get this
year. Pick the team or a location on the left to see its holidays.

## Add holidays

Select **Add holiday** on the team or location, or **Add holiday / leave** at the top and then
**Team or public holiday**.

1. Under **Who gets these days off?**, choose **A team** or **A location**, then pick which.
2. Fill in each **Holiday** with its **Start date**, **End date** and **Duration** (a full or
   half day). Use **Add holiday** for more rows.
3. Select **Add**.

Holidays are approved straight away, and Sup! announces them in the team's channel.

## Import public holidays

On a location, select **Import public holidays**, choose the year, and select **Import**. Sup!
adds that country's public holidays and observances, without announcing each one.

## Who can add holidays

Team managers add their team's holidays. Holiday admins add public holidays. Location holidays
are a paid feature.

## Holidays and follow-ups

On a holiday, [follow-ups](/docs/follow-ups/schedule#leave-and-public-holidays) skip the people
who are off.
