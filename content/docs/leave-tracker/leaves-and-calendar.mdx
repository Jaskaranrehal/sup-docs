## Leaves

The **Leaves** tab lists leave requests for the month or year, with each one's **Dates**,
**Member**, **Type** and **Status**.

![The Leaves tab: October's leave for the Engineering team, with dates, member, type and status](/images/leave-tracker/leaves.png)

- Switch between **Month** and **Year**, and use **Hide past** and **Show cancelled**.
- Managers can filter by member and export the list as a CSV file.
- Row actions let you edit, **Withdraw request**, **Cancel leave** or **Delete**, depending on
  the leave and your role.

Team and public holidays have [their own tab](/docs/leave-tracker/holidays).

## Calendar

The **Calendar** tab has two views:

- **Team timeline**: one row per person for the month, with their leave, public holidays and
  team holidays. Pending requests are hatched.
- **Calendar**: a month or year calendar of the team's leave and holidays.

![The Team timeline for November: Engineering's leave, pending requests, the team hack day and public holidays](/images/leave-tracker/calendar.png)

What you see follows the team's [visibility setting](/docs/leave-tracker/leave-types-and-policy#leave-policy):
approvers see all of their team's leave, and teammates see each other's approved leave if the
team allows it.

## Subscribe in your calendar app

Select **Subscribe** on the **Calendar** tab, then **Create my link**. Add the private link to
Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or Outlook to see the leave you can see in your team (pending
requests marked tentative) and your team and public holidays.

![The Subscribe to leave dialog, before a link is created](/images/leave-tracker/subscribe.png)

Calendar apps refresh subscriptions on their own schedule; Google can take up to a day. Anyone
with the link can see these events, so use **Turn off** or **New link** if it's shared by
mistake.

## Google Calendar sync

On a paid plan, you can also sync approved leave and holidays to a shared Google Calendar. Set
it up under **Integrations** in the dashboard.
