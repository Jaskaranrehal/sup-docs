---
title: Set up a team
description: Create a team, add its members and managers, and pick where its notifications go.
---

Leave is tracked per team. Each person belongs to one team at a time, and each team has its
own managers, rules and notification channel.

## Create a team

Only holiday admins can create teams (see [Who can do what](/docs/leave-tracker/access)).

<Steps>
<Step>
Open **Holidays / Leaves**, select the team picker at the top, then **Create a team**.
</Step>
<Step>
Give the team a **Team name** and pick its **Notification channel**: where requests, approvals
and announcements for this team are posted. On Slack, type the channel name and select
**Send test notification**.
</Step>
<Step>
Add **Members** and **Managers**, and set **Leaves available in days**: the annual leave each
member gets. Then select **Create**.
</Step>
</Steps>

People already in another team can't be added until they're taken off it.

## The team-ready checklist

After you create a team, Sup! shows **(team) is ready to take requests**: a checklist of the
team's settings, filled in with sensible defaults. It flags anything still missing, such as a
channel or members, and links straight to each setting. Select **Later** to finish it another
time.

## Members and managers

In **Settings**, open **Members** or **Managers**.

![Settings > Managers for the Engineering team, with what managers can do](/images/leave-tracker/settings-managers.png)

- **Members** are the people whose leave the team tracks. Select **Add members**, or
  **Remove** someone to take them off the team; their past leave stays on record.
- **Managers** approve the team's leave, record leave for team members, add team holidays and
  change the team's settings. A team needs at least one manager, and managers can be in any
  team.

When you add people to a team in a workspace with locations, Sup! asks them to set their
location so they get the right public holidays.

## Team settings

**Settings > Team settings** has the team's name, its notification channel, and
**Slack status on leave**: a default status Sup! sets on people's Slack profile for the length
of each approved leave. People can change it on each request. The default status is a paid
feature.

**Deactivate team** stops leave tracking for the team and keeps its history. You can activate
it again later.
