The Leave Tracker keeps your team's time off in one place. People request leave from Slack or
the dashboard, approvers approve it from a direct message, and Sup! tells the team who's away.
It also tracks each person's balance, and the team and public holidays they get.

Open it from **Holidays / Leaves** in the dashboard's left sidebar.

![The Holidays / Leaves Overview: who's away this week, upcoming holidays, three requests waiting, your balance and your upcoming leave](/images/leave-tracker/overview.png)

## How leave works

<Steps>
<Step>
**Request from Slack.** People open the Sup! app's **Home** tab and select
**Create holiday / leave**. [More on requesting leave](/docs/leave-tracker/request-leave).
</Step>
<Step>
**Approve from Slack.** The team's managers get a direct message and select **Approve** or
**Reject** right there. [More on approvals](/docs/leave-tracker/approvals).
</Step>
<Step>
**The team is told.** Sup! announces approved leave in the team's channel, sets the person's
Slack status while they're away, and pauses their follow-ups.
</Step>
</Steps>

Everything is also in the dashboard, under **Holidays / Leaves**.

## How it fits together

- **Teams**: everyone belongs to one team. A team has its own approvers, leave types, rules,
  holidays and notification channel. Use the team picker at the top to switch teams.
- **Locations**: where people are based, for public holidays. Locations are shared by the whole
  workspace, not tied to a team.
- **Managers** approve their team's leave and run its settings. They don't have to be in the
  team.

## The tabs

| Tab | What's there |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | Who's away this week, upcoming holidays, your balance, and requests waiting for you |
| **Leaves** | Every leave request for the month or year, with filters |
| **Approvals** | Requests waiting for your decision, and past decisions. Approvers only |
| **Team & public holidays** | The holidays your team and each location get |
| **Calendar** | A team timeline and a month or year calendar, and a calendar feed to subscribe to |
| **Reports** | Balances, leave by type, and exports |
| **Settings** | Team, members, managers, leave types, policy, blackout periods and locations. Managers only |

<Callout>
  The Leave Tracker is a paid feature. Workspaces on the older Pro plan get team holidays only:
  personal leave, approvals and balances need a plan that includes leave tracking.
</Callout>
