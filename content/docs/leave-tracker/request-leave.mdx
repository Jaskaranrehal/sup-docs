---
title: Request leave
description: For members, how to request time off straight from Slack, or from the dashboard, and change or withdraw a request.
---

The quickest way to request leave is from the Sup! app in Slack. You don't need to open the
dashboard.

## Request leave from Slack

<Steps>
<Step>
In Slack, open the **Sup!** app and go to its **Home** tab. Under **Your holidays / leaves**,
select **Create holiday / leave**.
</Step>
<Step>
In **Add holiday / leave**, pick the type under **Select type**, and add a **Reason** if you
like.
</Step>
<Step>
Choose the **Start date** and **End date**. For a single day you can pick a half day under
**Holiday duration**.
</Step>
<Step>
Optionally tick **Set Slack status** and choose a **Status emoji** and **Status message** to show
you're away while you're on leave.
</Step>
<Step>
Select **Add**.
</Step>
</Steps>

If the request breaks one of your team's rules (not enough notice, a reason needed, a blocked
[blackout period](/docs/leave-tracker/blackout-periods), or not enough balance), Slack tells you
before it's sent.

The **Home** tab also lists your holidays and leave for the next 60 days, with their status.

## What happens next

- If the leave type needs approval, your request is **Pending** and your team's managers get a
  Slack message to [approve or reject it](/docs/leave-tracker/approvals). You get a message when
  they decide.
- If it doesn't, or you're a manager of the team, it's approved straight away.
- Once approved, Sup! announces it in the team's channel and, if you chose a Slack status, sets
  it when your leave starts.

## Alternatively, request from the dashboard

You can also request leave from the Sup! dashboard. In Microsoft Teams, this is how you request
leave.

1. Open **Holidays / Leaves** and select **Add holiday / leave**.
2. Choose **Your leave**.

   ![The Add holiday / leave chooser: Your leave, Leave for a team member, and Team or public holiday](/images/leave-tracker/add-chooser.png)

3. Pick the **Type**, add a **Reason (optional)**, and choose the **Start date** and
   **End date**. For a single day, choose the **Duration**: a full day or a morning or evening
   half day.
4. Check the preview, then select **Add**.

![Add your leave: two days of Vacation/paid time off, with a preview showing it takes 2 days, the balance going from 16 to 14 days, and who else is away](/images/leave-tracker/request.png)

The preview shows how many days it takes, your balance before and after, holidays inside it,
and who else is away then. Rules that stop the request show in red and disable **Add**;
warnings show in amber and don't stop you.

## Change, withdraw or cancel

Open the request from **Leaves** or **Overview** in the dashboard.

- **Edit leave**: change a request that hasn't been approved yet. If you change the dates,
  your approvers are asked again.
- **Withdraw request**: take back a pending request.
- **Cancel leave**: cancel approved leave before it starts. Its days go back to your balance
  and your approvers are told.
