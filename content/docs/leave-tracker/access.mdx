## Roles

- **Holiday admins** create teams, manage locations and add public holidays. When access control
  is off, everyone is a holiday admin. When it's on, only admins with **Manage holidays / leaves**
  ticked in the dashboard's admin settings are, and they can manage every team.
- **Managers** (approvers) of a team approve its leave, record leave for its members, add its
  team holidays and change its settings. They don't have to be in the team.
- **Members** request their own leave, and change, withdraw or cancel it.

## Who can change leave

| Action | Who |
| --- | --- |
| Edit | Managers; or the person who asked, while it isn't approved and hasn't ended |
| Withdraw | The person who asked, while it's pending |
| Cancel | Managers; or the person who asked, for approved leave that hasn't started |
| Delete | Managers; or the person who asked, for future leave that isn't approved |

## Who sees whose leave

- Everyone sees team and public holidays, and all of their own leave.
- Approvers see all of their team's leave.
- Teammates see each other's approved leave only if the team's policy has
  **Members see each other's approved leave** on, and never the reason. Private leave types
  show as *Time off*.

## Plans

| Plan | What's included |
| --- | --- |
| Plans with leave tracking | Everything: personal leave, approvals, balances, location holidays, reports, Slack status and calendar sync |
| Older Pro plan | Team holidays only. The Overview, Leaves, Approvals and Reports tabs are hidden |
| No leave tracking | The Holidays / Leaves page explains the feature and offers an **Upgrade** button |
