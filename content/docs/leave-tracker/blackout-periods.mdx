---
title: Blackout periods
description: Mark dates the team keeps free of leave, such as a release week or a year-end freeze.
---

A blackout period is a run of dates a team wants to keep free of leave. Add them in
**Settings > Blackout periods**.

![Settings > Blackout periods: Release week (warn) and Year-end freeze (block)](/images/leave-tracker/settings-blackout.png)

Each period has a name, **Dates** and **When it applies**:

- **Block**: members can't request leave on these days.
- **Warn**: requests go through, with a warning to the member and the approver.

Approvers can still record leave on blackout days. Blackout periods also show in the
**Leaves** list, and in the approver's view of a request that falls in one.
