title: Locations
description: Say where people are based, so each person gets the right public holidays.
---

A location is a place people are based, with a country. Public holidays belong to a location,
so everyone based there gets them. Locations are shared by the whole workspace; they don't
belong to a team.

Holiday admins manage locations in **Settings > Locations**.

![Settings > Locations: the workspace's locations, the default location for new people, and the people based at one location](/images/leave-tracker/settings-locations.png)

## Add a location

Select **Add location**, enter a **Location name** and pick the **Country**. For a new
location you can also:

- **Make it the default location**: people who join the workspace start here.
- **Add everyone without a location**: they get this location's public holidays.

## Place people

Pick a location and select **Add members** to base people there. Each person has one location.
The **No location** entry lists people who get no public holidays yet.

People can also set their own location in **Settings > Profile**, under **Your location**.

## Default location

**Default location for new people** puts people who join the workspace at a location from day
one, so they get its public holidays. It doesn't move anyone already placed.

## Rename or delete

Use the location's menu to **Rename location** or **Delete location**. Deleting removes the
location and all its public holidays, and leaves its people without a location. It can't be
undone.
