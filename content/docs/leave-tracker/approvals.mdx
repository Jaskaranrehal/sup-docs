---
title: Approve leave
description: For managers and admins, how to approve or reject leave straight from Slack or Microsoft Teams, or from the Approvals tab.
---

Approvers don't need to open the dashboard to decide on leave. When someone in your team
requests leave, Sup! sends you a direct message in Slack (or Microsoft Teams), and you approve or
reject it right there.

## Approve or reject from Slack

The message from Sup! says *"(name) requested a leave"* and shows the **Member**, **Leave type**,
**Dates**, **Duration** and **Reason**, how many days they'll have left after approval, who else
is away then, and any policy warning, such as a blackout period.

<Steps>
<Step>
To approve, select **Approve**. The message changes to *"Leave request has been approved."*
</Step>
<Step>
To reject, select **Reject**. In **Reject leave**, add an optional **Reason for rejection**, then
select **Reject**. The message changes to *"Leave request has been rejected."*
</Step>
</Steps>

**View on dashboard** opens the request in the dashboard if you want more detail first.

The person who asked gets a Slack message with your decision, and your reason if you rejected
it. If they change the dates of a pending request, you get a new message saying they've modified
it.

Requests still waiting the next working day get a reminder at 10 AM, with a **Review requests**
button.

Microsoft Teams works the same way: the request arrives as a card with **Approve**, **Reject**
and **View on dashboard**.

## Alternatively, use the Approvals tab

To see everything waiting for you at once, open **Holidays / Leaves** in the dashboard and select
**Approvals**. The badge shows how many requests are waiting.

![The Approvals tab: three requests waiting, and Noah Kim's five days of vacation selected, with the balance after, a blackout warning and who else is off](/images/leave-tracker/approvals.png)

**Waiting for you** lists requests by team, soonest first. Select one to see its type and
reason, dates, **Balance after**, any policy warnings, and who's **Also off then**. Then select
**Approve** or **Reject**; when you reject, you can add a **Note** for the person. The next
waiting request opens automatically.

**Past** shows the requests you've decided. You can **Cancel leave** on approved leave from
there.

## The leave's own page

Select **Open leave** to see a request on its own page: its timeline (who asked, who decided,
and whether it was done in Slack or Teams), the person's balance, and whether their follow-ups
will pause.

![A pending leave's page: Noah Kim's vacation, five days, with the balance after approval, the blackout warning, details and timeline](/images/leave-tracker/leave-details.png)

## Who approves

A team's [managers](/docs/leave-tracker/set-up-a-team#members-and-managers) approve its leave.
If a team has no managers and your workspace uses access control, requests go to the holiday
admins, who can manage every team.

## Record leave for someone

Managers can add leave on someone's behalf: select **Add holiday / leave**, then
**Leave for a team member**. Leave you add as a manager is approved straight away.
