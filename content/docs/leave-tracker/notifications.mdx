title: What Sup! posts
description: The messages, digests and Slack statuses the Leave Tracker sends, and when.
---

## Direct messages

| Who | When | What |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Approvers | Someone requests leave or changes a pending request | The request, with **Approve**, **Reject** and **View on dashboard** |
| The person who asked | An approver decides | *"(approver) has approved your leave…"*, or declined, with the reason and an **Edit leave** link |
| The person on leave | Someone else cancels their leave | Who cancelled it, and why |
| Approvers | Someone withdraws or cancels their own leave | Who, which leave, and when |
| New team members | They're added to a team in a workspace with locations | A request to set their location, with **Set location** |

## In the team's channel

- **Announcements**: when leave is approved, or a holiday is added, Sup! posts
  *"Upcoming leave on (date)"* or *"Upcoming holiday on (date)"* with who, the type and the
  reason. Private leave types show as *Time off*, without a reason. If the leave is cancelled,
  the post changes to say so.
- **Daily digest**: at 8 AM on working days, *"Today's Leaves/Holidays"* lists who's off today.
- **Weekly digest**: at 6 PM on the last working day of the week,
  *"Next week's Leaves/Holidays"* lists next week's holidays and leave.

Public holidays are announced in the channels of every team with people at that location.

## Slack status

If someone chose a Slack status for their leave (or the team has a default), Sup! sets it on
their Slack profile when the leave starts and clears it when the leave ends or is cancelled.
People need to have given Sup! permission to update their Slack profile.

## Follow-ups

Approved full-day leave pauses that person's
[follow-ups](/docs/follow-ups/schedule#leave-and-public-holidays) for those days. Follow-ups
still run on half days.
