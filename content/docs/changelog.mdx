## 25 Oct 2026

**Improved Leave Tracker.** We've rebuilt the Leave Tracker. People still request leave from Slack or Microsoft Teams and approvers decide from a direct message, and around that there's now much more to help you plan time off. [Read the announcement](/docs/announcements/leave-tracker-overhaul).

{/* Sources: sup-backend 717eff65, fd1f85be, a6961674, 8f529cd9; sup-frontend 4d05f9ad, a7feee9e, 73fc2318. */}

![The Holidays / Leaves Overview: who's away this week, upcoming holidays, requests waiting, your balance and your upcoming leave](/images/leave-tracker/overview.png)

1. **Balances and a leave policy for each team.** Set an annual allowance and how many unused days carry over, and choose whether each leave type counts against the allowance, has its own balance, or isn't counted.
2. **Rules for each leave type.** Choose whether it needs approval, how much notice people must give, whether a reason is needed, whether half days are allowed, and whether it's private.
3. **An approvals inbox.** See every request waiting for you, with the balance after approval and who else is off. Approval messages in Slack and Microsoft Teams show this too.
4. **Withdraw, cancel, and a page for every leave.** People can withdraw a pending request or cancel approved leave until it starts, and each leave shows its timeline.
5. **Team and public holidays in one place.** Import a year of public holidays for a location in one go, and set a default location for people who join.
6. **Blackout periods and absence limits.** Keep dates like a release week free of leave, and get a warning when too many people would be off on the same day.
7. **A calendar you can subscribe to.** Add your team's leave and holidays to Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or Outlook.
8. **Reports.** See everyone's balance and leave by type, and export the year to Excel or CSV.
9. **Unpaid time off no longer counts against the annual allowance.** To count it again, set **Counts against** to **Annual allowance** for the type in **Settings > Leave types**.

Your teams, members, leave types and holidays carry over as they were, and the new rules stay off until you turn them on.
