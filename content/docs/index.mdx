Sup! runs your team's check-ins, leave tracking and forms inside Slack or Microsoft Teams. You set
things up in the [Sup! dashboard](https://app.supbot.com). Your team never has to leave chat:
Sup! messages them, and they answer, request leave and fill in forms right there.

## Get started

<Steps>
<Step>
**Log in to the dashboard.** Go to [app.supbot.com](https://app.supbot.com) and select
**Login with Slack** or **Login with Teams**. On Slack, if Sup! isn't in your workspace yet,
you're taken to add it. On Microsoft Teams, add the Sup! app to a team first.
</Step>
<Step>
**Check the basics.** In [Settings > Workspace](/docs/settings/workspace), make sure the
workspace timezone and working days are right. Everyone can set their own timezone in
[Settings > Profile](/docs/settings/profile).
</Step>
<Step>
**Start with what your team needs.** [Create a follow-up](/docs/follow-ups/create) for a daily
standup, [set up a team in the Leave Tracker](/docs/leave-tracker/set-up-a-team), or
[create a form](/docs/forms/create).
</Step>
<Step>
**Decide who runs Sup!.** By default, everyone in the workspace can change Sup!'s settings. To
limit that to a few admins, turn on [access control](/docs/settings/admins).
</Step>
</Steps>

## Slack and Microsoft Teams

Sup! works on both. A few things only work on Slack, and each page says so where it applies.
For example, conversational follow-ups, running a follow-up in each member's own timezone,
setting a Slack status while someone is on leave, and the referral program.

Questions? See the [FAQ](/docs/faq).
