*23 Nov 2026*

Forms used to be a separate purchase. They're now part of the Follow-ups plan, which is called
**Follow-ups & Forms** from today.

- **Already on the Follow-ups plan?** Forms are included from today. Your price doesn't change
  and there's nothing to turn on: select **Forms** in the dashboard's left sidebar.
- **Choosing a plan now?** Forms come with **Follow-ups & Forms** and with **All-in-one**. See
  [New plans and pricing](/docs/announcements/new-plans).

![The Forms list with three active forms, their descriptions, response counts and Active switches](/images/forms/list.png)

## What you can do with Forms

A form is a set of questions your team answers whenever they need to, from Slack or Microsoft
Teams: a lunch poll, an equipment request, an anonymous survey. Unlike a follow-up, it has no
schedule.

- **Share it once, or pin it** where people can find it.
  [More on sharing](/docs/forms/share).
- **Post responses to a channel**, or send them to the people who need to know.
- **Mark requests as done** with an approval flow.
  [More on responses and approvals](/docs/forms/responses).
