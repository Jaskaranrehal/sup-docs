*23 Nov 2026*

Sup! now has three plans. Each one is a single flat price for the whole workspace, set by how
many people are in it, so there are no separate products to add up.

If you already subscribe, nothing changes: you keep your plan and your price. See
[If you already subscribe](#if-you-already-subscribe).

![The Billing page's Choose a plan section: Follow-ups & Forms, Leave Tracker and All-in-one side by side, each with its price and what it includes](/images/announcements/plans.png)

## The plans

| Plan | What you get | Starts at |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Follow-ups & Forms** | Unlimited follow-ups and members, any schedule, reminders and summaries, mood tracking, stats and reports, custom bot messages and access control, and unlimited forms | $29 a month |
| **Leave Tracker** | Leave requests, approvals and balances, leave types and policies, team and public holidays, the calendar and reports, Slack status and Google Calendar sync | $49 a month |
| **All-in-one** | Everything in Follow-ups & Forms and everything in Leave Tracker | $69 a month |

Paying yearly saves up to 25%. For the price at your team size, see
[pricing](https://www.supbot.com/pricing/).

The Free plan is still there, and a new workspace still starts with a 14-day trial.

## Priced by team size

A plan's price depends on how many people are in your workspace:

- up to 100 people
- 101–250 people
- 251–500 people

**Billing** shows the price for your size, so there's nothing to pick. Workspaces of more than
500 people get a custom plan: select **Contact us** on a plan and we'll get back to you.

## Choose a plan

<Steps>
<Step>
Open **Billing** in the dashboard's left sidebar. With access control on, only admins with
**Manage billing** can change the plan.
</Step>
<Step>
Under **Choose a plan**, pick **Monthly** or **Yearly**.
</Step>
<Step>
Select **Choose Follow-ups & Forms**, **Choose Leave Tracker** or **Choose All-in-one**, and pay
in Stripe.
</Step>
</Steps>

Not sure which one fits? Open **Compare all features** under the plans to see them next to the
Free plan.

To move to another plan later, or between monthly and yearly, select **Change plan in Stripe**
on the plan you want. The change applies right away, and Stripe prorates what you've already
paid.

## If you already subscribe

Plans bought before 23 Nov 2026 carry on as they are, at the same price, with the same features.
**Billing** shows them with a **Legacy** label.

- **Manage in Stripe** still opens your card, invoices and cancellation.
- To move to one of the new plans, select **Contact us to switch** on that plan and send the
  request. We'll make the change for you.
- If you subscribe to Follow-ups, your plan now includes Forms at no extra cost. See
  [Forms now come with Follow-ups](/docs/announcements/forms-with-follow-ups).
