*25 Oct 2026*

We've rebuilt the Leave Tracker. The basics work the way your team is used to: people request
leave from Slack or Microsoft Teams, approvers decide from a direct message, and Sup! tells the
team who's away. Around that, there's now much more to help you plan time off.

Open it from **Holidays / Leaves** in the dashboard's left sidebar.

![The Holidays / Leaves Overview: who's away this week, upcoming holidays, three requests waiting, your balance and your upcoming leave](/images/leave-tracker/overview.png)

## What's new

### An overview of your week

**Overview** shows who's away each day this week, the holidays coming up, your balance and your
next leave. Approvers also see the requests waiting for them.

### Balances and a leave policy for each team

Set each team's **Annual allowance** and how many unused days **carry over** into the next year.
Each leave type counts against the annual allowance, has its own balance, or isn't counted.
Approvers can give one person a different allowance, or add or take away days, from
[Adjust a balance](/docs/leave-tracker/balances-and-reports#adjust-a-balance).
[More on leave types and policy](/docs/leave-tracker/leave-types-and-policy).

### Rules for each leave type

For each type, choose whether it needs approval, how much notice people must give, whether a
reason is needed, whether half days are allowed, and whether it's private, so teammates see only
"Time off". [More on leave types](/docs/leave-tracker/leave-types-and-policy#leave-types).

### An approvals inbox

**Approvals** lists every request waiting for you, by team, with the balance after approval, any
policy warning, and who else is off. Approve or reject, and the next request opens. **Past**
keeps the requests you've decided. The direct message approvers get in Slack or Microsoft Teams
now shows the days left after approval and who else is away, too.
[More on approvals](/docs/leave-tracker/approvals).

![The Approvals tab: three requests waiting, and Noah Kim's five days of vacation selected, with the balance after, a blackout warning and who else is off](/images/leave-tracker/approvals.png)

### Withdraw, cancel, and a page for every leave

People can withdraw a pending request, or cancel approved leave until it starts; approvers can
cancel any of their team's leave. Each leave has its own page with its timeline: who asked, who
decided, and whether it was done in Slack or Microsoft Teams.
[More on changing a request](/docs/leave-tracker/request-leave#change-withdraw-or-cancel).

### Team and public holidays in one place

The **Team & public holidays** tab lists your team's holidays and the public holidays of each
location. Import a year of public holidays for a location in one go, and set a default location
so people who join get the right holidays from day one.
[More on holidays](/docs/leave-tracker/holidays) and [locations](/docs/leave-tracker/locations).

![The Team & public holidays tab: the Engineering team's holidays, and the New York, London and Bengaluru locations](/images/leave-tracker/holidays.png)

### Blackout periods and absence limits

Mark dates the team keeps free of leave, such as a release week, and either block requests or
just warn. A team can also warn when too many people would be off on the same day.
[More on blackout periods](/docs/leave-tracker/blackout-periods).

### A calendar you can subscribe to

The **Calendar** tab has a team timeline and a month or year calendar. Select **Subscribe** to
add your team's leave and holidays to Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or Outlook with a private
link. [More on the calendar](/docs/leave-tracker/leaves-and-calendar).

### Reports

In **Reports**, approvers see everyone's balance and leave by type, and can export the year to
Excel or CSV. [More on balances and reports](/docs/leave-tracker/balances-and-reports).

### Settings in one place

A team's settings are now in **Holidays / Leaves > Settings**, in sections: team settings,
members, managers, leave types, policy, blackout periods and locations. Links to the old pages
still work. [More on setting up a team](/docs/leave-tracker/set-up-a-team).

## What changes for your existing teams

There's nothing to set up. Your teams, members, managers, leave types and holidays are where they
were, and the new rules stay off until you turn them on. A few things to know:

- **Unpaid time off no longer counts against the annual allowance**, so people who took unpaid
  leave have more days left. To count it again, open the type in **Settings > Leave types** and
  set **Counts against** to **Annual allowance**.
- **Members still see only their own leave** and the holidays they get. To let teammates see each
  other's approved leave, without reasons, turn on **Members see each other's approved leave** in
  the team's policy.
- **Allowance and carry-over changes count from the year you make them.** Earlier years keep
  their numbers, and a carry-over you set now first applies to the days carried into next year.
